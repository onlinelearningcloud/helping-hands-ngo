About Savithri Seva Sansthan

Savithri Seva Sansthan is a non-profit organization based in Ara, Bihar, working towards the upliftment of underprivileged communities. Founded in 2012 and officially registered in 2018, the organization is led by its founder, Mr. Dilip Kumar Verma.

Mission

To empower underprivileged communities by ensuring access to quality education, promoting environmental sustainability, and providing essential healthcare services.

Vision

To build a self-reliant and inclusive society where every individual has access to education, healthcare, and a clean environment, enabling them to live a dignified and sustainable life.
